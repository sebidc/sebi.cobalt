a little about sebi.cobalt

This is an independent shortcut to cobalt on meowing.de, inspired by its simple paste-and-save experience.

When you continue, your link is passed to meowing.de in the address fragment. Their website handles the download, available formats, and any browser challenge.

This page has no analytics and does not store your links. The receiving website has its own policies. We are not affiliated with cobalt or the meowing.de operator.
